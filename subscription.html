What happens after my free trial?

After your 3-day free trial ends, the AI Trading Engine pauses until your payment is confirmed. Pay securely with Packet Africa, then tell us you've paid — the XeroAI team confirms it and activates your account. Your account, settings, and history stay intact the whole time; only automation is paused.

Secure Subscription Verification

  • Payments are made on Packet Africa's secure checkout — XeroAI never sees or stores your card details.
  • Every payment is manually verified against the Packet Africa dashboard before your account is activated.
  • Clearing browser data, cookies, or cache will not reset your free trial or payment status.
  • Always enter your Account ID at checkout so your payment can be matched to your account.
  • Only a payment confirmed by the XeroAI team will unlock the AI Engine after your trial.

What happens after my free trial?

After your 5-day free trial expires, the AI Trading Engine will pause until your payment is confirmed. Transfer your subscription fee to the Opay account below, submit your receipt, and the account owner will review and approve it manually — your AI Engine unlocks as soon as that happens.

Secure Subscription Verification

  • Every payment is manually verified against the Opay business account before approval.
  • Clearing browser data, cookies, or cache will not reset your free trial or payment status.
  • Always include your Account ID as the transfer narration so your payment can be matched to your receipt.
  • Only a receipt confirmed and approved by the XeroAI team will unlock the AI Engine.
  • This protects the fairness and security of the XeroAI platform for all users.
